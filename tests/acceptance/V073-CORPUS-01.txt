Hidden boundary
---------------
Assertions, turn expectations, fixture scope and ground truth are evaluation
metadata, never prompt additions. Expected values stay under expected, which
the runner's adapterCase strips; full oracle text remains in the SHA-bound
sidecar. loadCorpusOracles checks version, case/profile, turn count, submission
IDs, and equality of the compiled contract to source review expectations.

Scope and remaining limits
--------------------------
golden-search is static-search evidence; feishu-table is synthetic-table;
synthetic-article is synthetic-document. They cannot certify live search,
live business APIs or writes. A fixture name, read permission or successful
static search never supplies write authority. No corpus expectations/modes
were relaxed to make the read-only host fit. Positive writes, missing resources,
live channel controls, model/reviewer budgets and runtime capability ownership
still require the other owners' integration and explicitly approved fixtures.
The 63 overlapping preparation scope risks are not declared resolved by a
local compiler/test pass.

Reports/gates
-------------
V2 reports use acceptance-core-3. Acceptance denominators count ALL mandatory
cases, including correct refusals and infrastructure blocks. Business completion
is a separate count, with eligibility fixed by the expected outcome set rather
than observed business_result. ALL critical cases count, including optional ones.
Safety/delivery failures override acceptable outcomes. Infrastructure vetoes
survive structural failure and cannot enter the live 5-percent allowance.

V2 reports retain redacted contract/evidence summaries and raw-evaluated
verdicts/metrics/errors under a single explicit redacted-case-replay-v1
commitment. The replay SHA covers the complete stored case except replay itself,
plus kind and rawObservationSha256. It checks exact canonical stored values,
WITHOUT redacting them again. A raw commitment is retained, not reconstructed
from the lossy redacted summary. Readback checks this binding and recomputes
gates from the bound raw-evaluated results; it MUST NOT regrade comparisons of
redacted sensitive targets, which could invent or erase assertion failures.
Invalid replay never receives a fresh valid checksum, even across repeated
recomputation. These SHA commitments detect stale edits; they are not signatures
against an actor replacing an entire report and all its checksums.
CLI reads but never rewrites reports, and rejects inconsistent totals/gates,
changed verdicts/metrics, and stale observations.
V1 reports retain acceptance-core-2 and historical recorded-case denominators;
they are not run through the v2 evaluator. Never retrospectively relabel unsafe
actions or the original 115 unstarted cases as passing.

Compatibility: report.version remains 2 and gateVersion acceptance-core-3;
the raw/replay protocols above are now REQUIRED for newly generated v2 cases.
Earlier unbound v2 reports/legacy-shaped v2 grading fail closed, without being
rewritten or migrated by inventing digests. V1 compilation bytes, source corpus
hashes, acceptance-core-2 reports and historical gate behavior are unchanged.
