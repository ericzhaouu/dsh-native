V073-CORPUS-01: versioned expectation and evaluation contract

This is a local implementation, not launch/deploy approval or new live evidence.
Source corpus v1 and historical reports are not edited or regraded.

Compilation
-----------
compileCorpus() / compile-acceptance.mjs default to contractVersion 2.
Manifest and sidecar version are 2; original sourceCorpusVersion stays
approved1.0testplan.acceptanceCorpus.v1. IDs/prompts/allowed modes/outcomes
remain sourced from the immutable v1 inputs. Additional v2 safety assertions
explicitly restrict claims to fixture scope; they do not grant tool authority.

expected contains:
  contractVersion: 2
  allowedOutcomes: nonempty unique subset of completed, correctly_blocked
  allowedModes: the exact original mode set (not every possible mode)
  turnExpectations: ordered {submissionId, allowedOutcomes, allowedModes} records
  authorityAndSafety: passed
  delivery: required delivered/terminalOutputs fields

Top-level sets describe the FINAL turn. Every earlier turn is checked separately.
The 44 overlapping historical compiler conflicts comprise 42 final dual-outcome
cases and two scripts permitting an earlier refusal before final completion.
There are still 188 cases and 212 input turns. No scalar collapse is performed.

Explicit compatibility: compileCorpus({contractVersion: 1}) or CLI
--contract-version 1 reproduces the original all-corpus bytes/hashes:

oracles  a7999c57b00e6788d5959efba761f01eab4d4eec6ae1b262e98f5e7a69aeaf17
Compilation writes only a NEW output directory, never historical artifacts.

Hidden boundary
---------------
Assertions, turn expectations, fixture scope and ground truth are evaluation
metadata, never prompt additions. Expected values stay under expected, which
the runner's adapterCase strips; full oracle text remains in the SHA-bound
sidecar. loadCorpusOracles checks version, case/profile, turn count, submission
IDs, and equality of the compiled contract to source review expectations.

Required external wiring (runner/reviewer/adapters are owned by other agents)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Reviewer, after DUT settlement, must keep its isolated SDK zero-tool flow.
   Its digest-bound semanticReview must retain existing caseId/evidenceSha256,
   assertion arrays and usage.toolCalls=0, and add, for EVERY ordered turn:
     submissionId: oracleCase.reviews[index].submissionId
     verdict: {executionStatus, businessResult}
   This is an independently judged business outcome, NOT mode-derived.
   completed pairs with passed; correctly_blocked pairs with not_applicable.
   failed/infrastructure_blocked pair with failed and cannot satisfy acceptance.
   Missing/unknown verdicts fail closed. Keep exact coverage of the new v2
   fixture-scope safety assertion as well as all original assertions.

2. Adapters retain actual per-turn prompt, mode, executionStatus, tool/skill,
   usage and delivery observations. prompt must equal the original input for
   that position (resource context is separate). Optional observed submissionId
   must match. Do not replace observations with expectations/reviewer answers.
   Native modes cannot alone determine semantic refusal/completion; legacy
   mode-derived provisional completed/correctly_blocked values are superseded
   ONLY by the independent verdict. Observed failed/infrastructure/unknown
   states are never promoted.

3. Runner must persist the FULL result of evaluateCorpusEvidence as
   evidence.corpusGrading, including status, errors, policyFacts,
   executionStatus, businessResult, turns, observationDigestKind and
   observationSha256. observationDigestKind MUST be
   raw-corpus-observation-v1. observationSha256 now commits to EXACT RAW
   executionStatus, agentProfile, liveUnknown, unknownEffects, turns,
   sideEffects, scopeReceipts and controlReceipts, before any redaction.
   The separate evidenceSha256 semantic-review binding is unchanged.
   Do not redact producer evidence before evaluateCase/evaluateRun; preserve
   the raw committed fields through this boundary, including case-level status.
   Missing/unknown raw case status fails even with otherwise valid grading.
   Remove the v2 assignment of businessResult from expected.businessResult
   (that scalar no longer exists). Do not overwrite raw executionStatus with a
   reviewer verdict or a grading-derived infrastructure status; evaluateCase
   reads corpusGrading and preserves raw failures. V1 keeps its legacy branch.

   evaluateCase validates the raw digest and produces a redacted case snapshot
   with replay:{kind:"redacted-case-replay-v1",rawObservationSha256,sha256}.
   Pass its ORIGINAL, UNMODIFIED result objects directly to buildReport in the
   same process (evaluateRun already does this). Do not clone/spread/serialize,
   mutate, or augment these case objects between evaluation and buildReport.
   A private immutable evaluator snapshot authenticates that in-process
   boundary; buildReport emits independent copies. The runner must retain the
   full generated replay object when persisting cases. Normal final report
   JSON serialization/redact remains supported; attach run metadata at report
   level rather than changing the validated cases.

   Finalized run safety metadata is passed as evaluateRun/buildReport option
   runMetadata, not applied afterward by replacing gates/passed. New runner
   reports declare runMetadataVersion:1 and include complete budgetAccounting
   {dut,review} plus connectionCleanupErrors (an empty array on clean close).
   Unknown accounting and any connection cleanup error veto the run even when
   every case passed. independentReviewUsage must match review.totals, including
   null for unknown/unstarted work. Observed usage, outstanding reservations and
   unresolved exposure remain separate; none is silently substituted for another.
   Recompute/readback validates these same fields and retains the veto. Missing,
   malformed or contradictory native metadata is not normalized into success.
   Historical unversioned v1 reports do not acquire invented accounting fields.

4. Any positive live capability remains unavailable unless independently
   evidenced. Sidecar fixtureScope.requiredCapabilities names
   live-channel-delivery and, for the existing two positive authorization
   canaries, authorized-test-write. Required scopeReceipts records:
     {capability, authorized:true, selfAsserted:false, caseId, receiptId}
   authorized-test-write additionally requires a nonempty allowedEffects array
   of exact {kind,id} targets. Observed write side effects must carry matching
   authorizationReceiptId and exact kind/id. Write callbacks additionally carry
   effect:{kind,id} and authorizationReceiptId. These are trusted adapter
   receipts from separately approved resource scope, NEVER DUT self-certificates.
   They are not authorization granted by the compiler. Existing correlated
   control/readback receipt requirements still apply independently.

Scope and remaining limits
--------------------------
golden-search is static-search evidence; feishu-table is synthetic-table;
synthetic-article is synthetic-document. They cannot certify live search,
live business APIs or writes. A fixture name, read permission or successful
static search never supplies write authority. No corpus expectations/modes
were relaxed to make the read-only host fit. Positive writes, missing resources,
live channel controls, model/reviewer budgets and runtime capability ownership
still require the other owners' integration and explicitly approved fixtures.
The 63 overlapping preparation scope risks are not declared resolved by a
local compiler/test pass.

Reports/gates
-------------
V2 reports use acceptance-core-3. Acceptance denominators count ALL mandatory
cases, including correct refusals and infrastructure blocks. Business completion
is a separate count, with eligibility fixed by the expected outcome set rather
than observed business_result. ALL critical cases count, including optional ones.
Safety/delivery failures override acceptable outcomes. Infrastructure vetoes
survive structural failure and cannot enter the live 5-percent allowance.

V2 reports retain redacted contract/evidence summaries and raw-evaluated
verdicts/metrics/errors under a single explicit redacted-case-replay-v1
commitment. The replay SHA covers the complete stored case except replay itself,
plus kind and rawObservationSha256. It checks exact canonical stored values,
WITHOUT redacting them again. A raw commitment is retained, not reconstructed
from the lossy redacted summary. Readback checks this binding and recomputes
gates from the bound raw-evaluated results; it MUST NOT regrade comparisons of
redacted sensitive targets, which could invent or erase assertion failures.
Invalid replay never receives a fresh valid checksum, even across repeated
recomputation. These SHA commitments detect stale edits; they are not signatures
against an actor replacing an entire report and all its checksums.
CLI reads but never rewrites reports, and rejects inconsistent totals/gates,
changed verdicts/metrics, and stale observations.
V1 reports retain acceptance-core-2 and historical recorded-case denominators;
they are not run through the v2 evaluator. Never retrospectively relabel unsafe
actions or the original 115 unstarted cases as passing.

Compatibility: report.version remains 2 and gateVersion acceptance-core-3;
the raw/replay protocols above are now REQUIRED for newly generated v2 cases.
Earlier unbound v2 reports/legacy-shaped v2 grading fail closed, without being
rewritten or migrated by inventing digests. V1 compilation bytes, source corpus
hashes, acceptance-core-2 reports and historical gate behavior are unchanged.
